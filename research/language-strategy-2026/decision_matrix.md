# Decision Matrix — Plixfy Language Selection

**Compiled:** 2026-06-02
**Method:** Each language scored 1-10 on seven dimensions. Dimensions weighted by plixfy's strategic constraints (solo founder, Playgama partner, Arabic base, mobile-first MENA core). Final score is weighted sum out of 100.

---

## Weight Rationale

| Dimension | Weight | Why this weight |
|---|---:|---|
| **Competition Difficulty (inverted)** | 25% | Highest weight. A solo founder cannot out-content/out-link DA-85 incumbents. Where you can rank is the binding constraint. |
| **RPM Rate** | 20% | Direct multiplier on revenue per visitor. But weight is not higher because volume × RPM matters and volume markets can outperform. |
| **Cultural/Religious Fit with Arabic Base** | 15% | Plixfy's content sensibility, branding palette, and existing Playgama catalog selection are already tuned for MENA. Lower friction means faster time-to-revenue. |
| **TikTok/Social Funnel Strength** | 15% | The non-SEO acquisition channel that matters in 2025-2026. AI Overviews are eating SEO CTR; social is the hedge. |
| **Market Size (Speakers × Internet Penetration)** | 10% | Important but discounted because you can't access the whole market — only your slice of it. |
| **Translation Feasibility for Solo Founder** | 10% | Matters for bandwidth, not strategic. LLM translation makes most languages feasible at acceptable quality. |
| **24-Month Growth Trajectory** | 5% | Lower weight because language-level growth is dwarfed by execution variance at solo-founder scale. |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

---

## Scoring (1-10, higher = better)

### Score definitions

- **Competition Difficulty (inverted)**: 10 = first page is weak, opportunity wide open; 1 = fortress of DA-85+ incumbents, no new entrant has broken in
- **RPM Rate**: 10 = ≥$5 RPM proven by operators; 1 = ≤$0.20 or unmonetizable
- **Cultural Fit Arabic Base**: 10 = Muslim-majority, shared values, content overlap; 1 = no overlap or actively dissonant
- **TikTok Funnel Strength**: 10 = top-3 global TikTok market with active gaming creators; 1 = TikTok banned or weak
- **Market Size**: 10 = 200M+ speakers with high internet penetration; 1 = small or fragmented
- **Translation Feasibility**: 10 = trivial (English-Arabic LLM is great); 1 = niche language with poor LLM coverage
- **24-Month Trajectory**: 10 = strong tailwinds (gaming growth, mobile, demographic); 1 = declining or sanctioned

## Override of Matrix: The Filipino Trap

The decision matrix flags Filipino at **57.5** — equal to Turkish, higher than Spanish — purely because the competition dimension scores so high (no dedicated Tagalog portal). **This is a measurement artifact.**

There is no Tagalog portal because there is no Tagalog gaming-search demand. Filipinos with internet access have English fluency rates above 90%. They search "free games" not "libreng laro." Adding a fil-PH locale to plixfy does not unlock the PH market; it just adds a UI layer that the same users would have visited anyway via English.

**Net effect of Filipino addition:** zero new addressable users, ~30% translation/maintenance overhead.

**Lesson:** A "low competition" score is only valuable if there is also demand. The matrix doesn't separately measure demand — it's bundled into Market Size. For Filipino, the right read is: Market Size should be **2**, not 5, because the addressable browser-game-via-Tagalog audience is essentially zero.

**Corrected Filipino score with Market Size = 2: 54.5 → still flagged. Treat the matrix as advisory and apply this override.**

## The Three-Language Recommendation

Based on the matrix, founder constraints, and qualitative judgment:

### **Primary: Arabic** (current base — deepen, do not fragment)
### **Secondary: Indonesian** (add at the right milestone — see roadmap in main report)
### **Tertiary: Turkish** *or* **English** (decision deferred to month 12-15 based on actual traffic data)

The Tertiary choice is intentionally deferred because:
- **Turkish** wins on cultural fit and SEO accessibility but loses badly on RPM (brutal lira economics)
- **English** wins on RPM and addressable market but loses on competition (fortress)
- The right call depends on whether plixfy's Arabic + Indonesian traction proves you can *win in low-RPM volume markets* (→ Turkish) or *needs higher RPM per visitor to break even* (→ English-as-long-tail-only, not as head-term play)

This is the right kind of decision to defer — wait for 6-12 months of own-data before committing the 3rd-language translation/maintenance budget.

---

## Confidence Caveats

- **The matrix is advisory, not algorithmic.** No matrix can capture all of: ad-network business-development reality, Playgama's specific catalog cultural fit, your personal language fluency, or holiday/Ramadan traffic seasonality.
- **Competition scores rest on inferred DA estimates** and qualitative SERP reads — not direct Moz/Ahrefs lookups. Treat as relative rankings, not absolute values.
- **RPM scores cluster in the 2-6 band for most languages** because real-operator data for non-English geos is thin. The Persian 0 and English 6 are the most defensible; the middle band is best-effort.
- **Trajectory scores are 24-month forecasts** — actively softer than the other dimensions.
